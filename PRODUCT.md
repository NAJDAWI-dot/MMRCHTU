# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Everyone in the hall on MMRC 26 competition day, at Al Hussein Technical University in Amman:

- **Spectators and families** on their phones between runs, in bright hall light, often one-handed and half-listening to the announcer. They want to know where their team stands and when it runs next, in one glance.
- **Competitors** checking their slot, the call queue, the rules on the day and whether they went through.
- **The hall screen audience**, reading a projected screen from ten metres away.
- **Organisers at Day HQ** on laptops, running check-in, scoring, the bracket, announcements and the reveal under time pressure.
- **Judges at the maze** with a tablet, timing runs and recording the cell a failed run reached.

The main site (registration, rules, the game) is the brand surface; the competition-day site under `/day` is the product surface this file describes.

## Product Purpose

The day site is the live record of the competition: who has run, the qualifying table, the knockout bracket, what is on the maze now and what is next, and every team's journey. Day HQ is where the organisers feed it. Success is a parent finding their team's place in two seconds, a judge saving a sheet without thinking, and the hall screen making the room cheer at the reveal.

## Brand Personality

Precise, quick, proud. A student robotics chapter running a proper race: the confidence of a timing board, the warmth of a home event. Plain words, short sentences, no hype, no em dashes. The maze is the hero: its posts, walls and cells are the vocabulary of the interface.

## Anti-references

- AI landing-page grammar: pill badges over a giant headline, four stat cards, an uppercase eyebrow over every section, identical icon-card grids, a closing logo card.
- Soft rounded cards with drop shadows stacked everywhere, glass bars, gradient text, blobs and glows used as decoration.
- Esports template chrome (angled neon panels, fake HUD brackets).
- Cream "paper" backgrounds chosen for warmth by default.

## Design Principles

1. **The maze is the grid.** Posts, walls and cells structure the page; decoration that is not part of the maze earns its place or goes.
2. **A glance, then depth.** Every screen answers its question at a glance (where is my team, what is on now), and opens for detail on a tap.
3. **Numbers are the heroes.** Times and scores are set like a timing board: tabular, aligned, never jumpy.
4. **Motion reports the race.** Things move when the competition moves (a place changes, a run lands, a round is revealed), not to decorate.
5. **Same words everywhere.** Public site, hall screen, HQ and the judge tablet name things the same way.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: body text 4.5:1 and large text 3:1 in both themes; focus always visible; targets at least 44px on touch; nothing conveyed by colour alone (winners carry a tick, places carry numbers); every animation has a reduced-motion fallback; the hall screen is readable from the back of the room.
