## Eligibility

MMRC 26 is open to all currently enrolled university students, individually or in teams of
up to four. Each team may enter at most one micromouse.

## The maze

The competition maze is a 16×16 grid of 18cm cells with 12cm walls, built to IEEE micromouse
standard dimensions. The start cell is in a corner; the goal is a 2×2 block at the maze center.

## Runs

Each team gets up to five runs. A run ends when the mouse reaches the goal, hits the time
limit (10 minutes), or the team retires the run. The fastest successful run counts.

## Scoring

- **Search runs** may explore freely and are not timed against the leaderboard.
- **Speed runs** must use only cells already discovered on a prior run for that team.
- Final ranking is by best speed-run time; ties are broken by best search-run time.

## Robot rules

- Maximum footprint: 25cm × 25cm at any rotation.
- No jumping, climbing, or damaging maze walls.
- Autonomous operation only — no remote control or manual intervention once a run starts.

## Conduct

Unsportsmanlike conduct, maze tampering, or rule violations may result in disqualification at
the judges' discretion. Judges' decisions are final.
