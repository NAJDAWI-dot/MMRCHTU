## Do I need to be an IEEE member to compete?

No. MMRC 26 is open to all university students; IEEE RAS HTU membership is not required.

## Can I compete alone?

Yes — teams range from one to four members.

## What hardware can I use?

Any autonomous micromouse robot that meets the footprint and maze-standard requirements in
the [rulebook](/rules). Kits and fully custom builds are both welcome.

## Is there a registration fee?

No, MMRC 26 is free to enter for all registered teams.

## Where can I follow live results?

The homepage live counters and the schedule page will be updated throughout the event; a full
results/news section is coming in a future site update.
