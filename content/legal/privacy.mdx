> **Who "we" are.** In this document, "we", "us" and "our" mean the IEEE
> Robotics and Automation Society, HTU Student Chapter ("IEEE RAS HTU"), which
> organizes MMRC 26. "You" means you, the reader.

Last updated 13 September 2026.

This policy explains what the MMRC 26 website collects, why we collect it, and
what we do with it. It covers mmrchtu.tech and the competition run by the IEEE
Robotics and Automation Society, HTU Student Chapter.

## What we collect

**When you register a team.** The team name; the email address of the person
registering; and for each member their first and last name, email address,
WhatsApp number, university, major, IEEE membership status and membership
number. We also keep what you write about your technical experience and why you
are entering, the membership tier you selected, and when you accepted these
policies.

**When you pay.** The CliQ transaction reference you report, the amount you say
you transferred, and the screenshot you upload as proof.

**When you ask a question.** The question, and your email address if you give
one so we can reply.

**When you join a mailing list.** Your email address and, if you give it, your
name.

**When you play the game.** The name you enter on the leaderboard and your score.
You can use a nickname if you'd rather not be identified. Nobody checks it.

**Automatically.** Standard server and hosting logs, including IP addresses. We
keep them for a short time to run the site and to stop abuse of the registration
and payment forms.

**When you open a page.** Vercel Web Analytics counts page views: which page you
opened, which site linked you to it, and your general device type and country.
It sets no cookie and stores nothing that could recognise you later, here or on
any other site. It counts visits with a value calculated from the request itself,
and that value changes every day. Nothing you type into a form on this site is
sent to it.

**In your own browser.** The site keeps a few things in your browser's local
storage, on your own device. None of them are sent anywhere, and only this site
can read them:

- `mmrc26.registration.draft`: your unfinished registration, saved as you type
  so you don't lose it if you refresh the page, close the tab or leave to make
  your bank transfer. It holds text only. Your payment screenshot is never saved
  in your browser; it uploads only when you submit. This entry is deleted as soon
  as your registration is recorded.
- `mmrc26-theme`: whether you chose the light or dark theme.
- `mmrc26-readiness`: the boxes you ticked on the readiness checklist.
- `mmrcClassicHi` and `mmrcFpHi`: your best score in each Pac Mouse mode.
- `pacMousePlayerName`: the name you last entered on the leaderboard, so you
  don't have to type it again.

You can remove all of them at any time by clearing your site data.

**Cookies.** No cookie is set on the public pages of this site, and that includes
the page view counting described above. The only cookie we use is the sign-in
session for the admin area, and it is set only when a member of the organizing
committee logs in. We don't track anything you do on the public pages with
cookies.

**An offline copy.** A service worker saves copies of pages and images in your
browser so the site still opens on a weak connection. It holds no personal data,
and clearing your site data removes it.

We use no advertising trackers, and we don't sell anything to anybody.

## Why we collect it

- To run the competition: contacting teams, checking eligibility, setting up the
  brackets and reaching teams quickly on the day.
- To take and verify the registration fee and match payments against the
  chapter's bank statement.
- To answer the questions you send.
- To email registered teams about MMRC, such as schedule changes, rule changes
  and results.
- To keep the site running and protect it from abuse.
- To see which pages people read, so we can build the next edition of this site
  around what they came for.

## Who else sees it

We use these service providers, and only these:

- **Vercel**: hosts the website, stores uploaded files including payment
  screenshots, and counts page views as described above.
- **Supabase**: hosts the database that stores registrations.
- **Resend**: sends the chapter's email.

Each of them processes this data only on our instructions and only to provide
their service. Apart from them, we share personal data only when the law
requires it, and with IEEE when we need to verify a membership number.

Some of these providers operate outside Jordan, so your data may be stored or
processed abroad.

## Payment screenshots

A screenshot from your banking app often shows more than the transfer, such as
account numbers, balances and other transactions. Please crop it to the
confirmation if you can.

We store these files privately, use them only to verify your payment, and delete
them once the competition is over and all payments are reconciled.

## What is public

The competition gallery, published results and the game leaderboard are public.
Everything you submit through the registration form is private, and only the
organizing committee can see it.

## How long we keep it

- Registration and payment records: for the competition and one year after it,
  for our accounts and to invite past teams to the next edition.
- Payment screenshots: deleted once the competition is over and payments are
  reconciled.
- Mailing list entries: until you ask to be removed.
- Questions sent through the FAQ form: until we answer them, and for a
  reasonable time afterwards.
- Server logs: for the short period our hosting providers keep them.

## Your choices

You can ask to see what we hold about you, have anything wrong corrected, have
your data deleted, or be removed from a mailing list. If you withdraw your
consent for us to hold your registration, your team is withdrawn from the
competition, because we can't run an entry we have no record of.

To do any of these, reply to any email we have sent you, or use the [FAQ
page](/faq).

## Children

MMRC 26 is for university students, and we don't knowingly collect data from
children. If you think we have, tell us and we will delete it.

## Changes

We may update this policy. If we make an important change, we will email
registered teams. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
