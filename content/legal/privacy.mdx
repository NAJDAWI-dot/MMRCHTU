This explains what the MMRC 26 website collects, why, and what we do with it. It
covers mmrchtu.tech and the competition run by the IEEE Robotics and Automation
Society, HTU Student Chapter.

## What we collect

**When you register a team.** Team name; the email address of the person
registering; and for each member their first and last name, email address,
WhatsApp number, university, major, IEEE membership status and membership
number. We also keep what you write about your technical experience and your
motivation for entering, the membership tier you selected, and the fact and time
of your accepting these policies.

**When you pay.** The CliQ transaction reference you report, the amount you say
you transferred, and the screenshot you upload as proof.

**When you ask a question.** The question, and your email address if you give one
so we can reply.

**When you join a mailing list.** Your email address and, if you give it, your
name.

**When you play the game.** The name you enter on the leaderboard and your score.
Use a nickname if you would rather not be identifiable — nothing checks it.

**Automatically.** Standard server and hosting logs, including IP addresses,
kept briefly to keep the site running and to rate-limit abuse of the
registration and payment forms.

**In your own browser.** While you are part-way through registering, what you
have typed is saved in your browser's local storage, on your device, so that
leaving the page to make your bank transfer does not lose it. None of it reaches
us until you submit your payment, and it is erased from your browser as soon as
your registration is recorded. Clearing your site data removes it at any time.

We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell anything to anybody.

## Why we collect it

- To run the competition: contacting teams, checking eligibility, seating the
  brackets, and reaching a team quickly on the day.
- To take and verify the registration fee, and to match payments against the
  chapter's bank statement.
- To answer questions you send us.
- To email registered teams about MMRC — schedule changes, rule amendments, and
  results.
- To keep the site available and resistant to abuse.

## Who else sees it

We use a small number of service providers, and only these:

- **Vercel** — hosts the website and stores uploaded files, including payment
  screenshots.
- **Supabase** — hosts the database where registrations are stored.
- **Resend** — sends our email.

Each of them processes this data on our instructions in order to provide their
service. Beyond them, we share personal data only where we are legally required
to, and with IEEE bodies where it is necessary to verify a membership claim.

Some of these providers operate outside Jordan, so your data may be stored or
processed abroad.

## Payment screenshots

A screenshot of a banking confirmation usually shows more than the transaction —
account numbers, balances, other transfers. Crop it to just the confirmation if
you can.

We store these files privately, use them only to verify your payment, and delete
them once the competition has concluded and all payments are reconciled.

## What is public

The competition gallery, published results, and the game leaderboard are public.
Everything you submit through the registration form is not — it is visible only
to the organizing committee.

## How long we keep it

- Registration records and payment records: for the competition and one year
  afterwards, for accounting and for inviting past entrants to the next edition.
- Payment screenshots: deleted once the competition concludes and payments are
  reconciled.
- Mailing list entries: until you ask to be removed.
- Questions sent through the FAQ form: until answered and for a reasonable period
  afterwards.
- Server logs: a short period, as our hosting providers retain them.

## Your choices

Write to us and you can ask to see what we hold about you, correct anything
wrong, have your data deleted, or be removed from a mailing list. Withdrawing
consent for us to hold your registration means withdrawing your team from the
competition, since we cannot run an entry we hold no record of.

To do any of these, reply to any email we have sent you or use the [FAQ
page](/faq).

## Children

MMRC 26 is for university students. We do not knowingly collect data from
children. If you believe we have, tell us and we will delete it.

## Changes

We may update this policy. If we change it materially, we will email registered
teams. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
