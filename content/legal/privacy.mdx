This explains what the MMRC 26 website collects, why, and what is done with it.
It covers mmrchtu.tech and the competition run by the IEEE Robotics and
Automation Society, HTU Student Chapter.

## What is collected

**When you register a team.** Team name; the email address of the person
registering; and for each member their first and last name, email address,
WhatsApp number, university, major, IEEE membership status and membership
number. What you write about your technical experience and your motivation for
entering is also kept, along with the membership tier you selected and the fact
and time of your accepting these policies.

**When you pay.** The CliQ transaction reference you report, the amount you say
you transferred, and the screenshot you upload as proof.

**When you ask a question.** The question, and your email address if you give one
so that a reply can be sent.

**When you join a mailing list.** Your email address and, if you give it, your
name.

**When you play the game.** The name you enter on the leaderboard and your score.
Use a nickname if you would rather not be identifiable — nothing checks it.

**Automatically.** Standard server and hosting logs, including IP addresses,
kept briefly to keep the site running and to rate-limit abuse of the
registration and payment forms.

**In your own browser.** While you are part-way through registering, what you
have typed is saved continuously in your browser's local storage, on your own
device, as you type it — so that refreshing the page, closing the tab, or
leaving to make your bank transfer does not lose it. None of it leaves your
device until you submit your payment, and it is erased from your browser as soon
as your registration is recorded. Clearing your site data removes it at any time.

One further note is kept in local storage once you have finished registering, so
that the animation at the end plays only once. It records nothing about you and
never leaves your device.

No advertising trackers are used, and nothing is sold to anybody.

## Why it is collected

- To run the competition: contacting teams, checking eligibility, seating the
  brackets, and reaching a team quickly on the day.
- To take and verify the registration fee, and to match payments against the
  chapter's bank statement.
- To answer questions you send.
- To email registered teams about MMRC — schedule changes, rule amendments, and
  results.
- To keep the site available and resistant to abuse.

## Who else sees it

A small number of service providers are used, and only these:

- **Vercel** — hosts the website and stores uploaded files, including payment
  screenshots.
- **Supabase** — hosts the database where registrations are stored.
- **Resend** — sends the chapter's email.

This data is processed by each of them only as instructed, in order to provide
their service. Beyond them, personal data is shared only where required by law,
and with IEEE bodies where it is necessary to verify a membership claim.

Some of these providers operate outside Jordan, so your data may be stored or
processed abroad.

## Payment screenshots

A screenshot of a banking confirmation usually shows more than the transaction —
account numbers, balances, other transfers. Crop it to just the confirmation if
you can.

These files are stored privately, are used only to verify your payment, and are
deleted once the competition has concluded and all payments are reconciled.

## What is public

The competition gallery, published results, and the game leaderboard are public.
Everything you submit through the registration form is not — it is visible only
to the organizing committee.

## How long it is kept

- Registration records and payment records: for the competition and one year
  afterwards, for accounting and for inviting past entrants to the next edition.
- Payment screenshots: deleted once the competition concludes and payments are
  reconciled.
- Mailing list entries: until you ask to be removed.
- Questions sent through the FAQ form: until answered and for a reasonable period
  afterwards.
- Server logs: a short period, as the hosting providers retain them.

## Your choices

Anything held about you can be seen on request; anything wrong can be corrected;
your data can be deleted; and you can be removed from a mailing list. Withdrawing
consent for your registration to be held means withdrawing your team from the
competition, since an entry that is not on record cannot be run.

To do any of these, reply to any email you have been sent, or use the [FAQ
page](/faq).

## Children

MMRC 26 is for university students. Data is not knowingly collected from
children. If you believe it has been, say so and it will be deleted.

## Changes

This policy may be updated. If it is changed materially, registered teams are
emailed. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
