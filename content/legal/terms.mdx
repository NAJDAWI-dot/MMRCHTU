These terms cover the MMRC 26 website at mmrchtu.tech and entry to the Maze
Solver Robot Competition 2026, run by the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society,
HTU Student Chapter. Registering a team means the person registering has read
these terms and accepts them on behalf of every member of that team.

## Who can enter

MMRC 26 is open to university students. A team is one to three people, and each
member must be able to show valid student identification if asked. One person
registers on behalf of the team and becomes the point of contact — everything is
sent to that address, so it needs to be one somebody reads.

You may not enter the same people under two team names, and you may not
register a team without the knowledge of the people named in it.

## Registration and your place in the competition

Registering is not the same as having a place. A registration is complete when
the fee has been paid and verified. Until then your team is recorded but not
confirmed.

Registration may be closed at any time, including before an advertised deadline,
if the competition fills. If your team cannot be given a place after you have
paid, the fee is refunded in full — see the [Payment & Refund
Policy](/legal/payment-refund-policy).

The information you give has to be accurate. If a team is found to have
misrepresented its members, their student status, or their IEEE membership in
order to pay a lower fee, the difference may be asked for, or the team
withdrawn.

## The competition itself

The rules of the competition — the maze, the robot specification, the run
format, scoring, and disqualification — live in the [rulebook](/rules) and form
part of these terms. The rulebook may be amended before competition day;
material changes are emailed to registered teams.

Your robot must be present and pass inspection on competition day. A team that
does not appear forfeits its place, and the fee is not refunded.

Judging decisions are final.

## Your robot and your work

Your robot and its code remain yours. No ownership is claimed over anything you
build.

By entering, you give permission for the event to be photographed and filmed,
including your team and your robot, and for those images to be used to publicise
MMRC and the chapter. If you would prefer not to appear, say so in writing before
competition day and it will be accommodated as far as possible — though no
promise can be given that you will be absent from every wide shot of a
competition hall.

## Using this website

Use the site for what it is for. Do not attempt to break into the administration
area, submit automated registrations, upload anything that is not what it claims
to be, or interfere with other people's use of the site.

Anyone who does may have their content removed and their service refused.

## What is not promised

MMRC 26 is run by a student chapter, on a volunteer basis. The site is provided
as it is. It is kept accurate and available as far as possible, but no guarantee
is given that it will be uninterrupted, that every date on it is final, or that
it is free of errors.

No liability is accepted for indirect or consequential loss. Nothing here limits
liability that cannot be limited by law, including for death or personal injury
caused by negligence.

## Changing these terms

These terms may be updated. If something is changed that materially affects
registered teams, the address each team registered with is emailed. The version
in force for your registration is the one recorded at the moment you accepted
it.
