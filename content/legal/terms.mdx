## Who can enter

MMRC 26 is open to university students. A team has one to three people, and each
member must be able to show valid student ID if asked. One person registers the
team and becomes our point of contact. We send everything to that address, so
make sure someone reads it.

You can't enter the same people under two team names, and you can't register a
team without the knowledge of the people in it.

## Registration and your place in the competition

Registering doesn't guarantee a place. Your registration is complete once we have
received and verified your fee. Until then, your team is on record but not
confirmed.

We may close registration at any time, including before an advertised deadline,
if the competition is full. If we can't give your team a place after you have
paid, we refund your fee in full. See the [Payment & Refund
Policy](/legal/payment-refund-policy).

The information you give us must be accurate. If a team gives false details about
its members, their student status or their IEEE membership to pay a lower fee, we
may ask for the difference or withdraw the team.

## The competition itself

The competition rules, covering the maze, the robot specification, the run
format, scoring and disqualification, are in the [rulebook](/rules) and form
part of these terms. We may change the rulebook before competition day, and we
will email registered teams about any important change.

Your robot must be at the venue and pass inspection on competition day. If your
team doesn't show up, you lose your place and we don't refund the fee.

The judges' decisions are final.

## Your robot and your work

Your robot and its code belong to you. We claim no ownership of anything you
build.

By entering, you agree that we may photograph and film the event, including your
team and your robot, and use those images to promote MMRC and the chapter. If you
would rather not appear, tell us in writing before competition day and we will do
our best. We can't promise you won't show up in a wide shot of the hall.
