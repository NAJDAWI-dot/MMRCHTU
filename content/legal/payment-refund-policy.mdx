This policy explains what the registration fee is, how to pay it, how it is
checked, and when it is given back.

## The fee

MMRC 26 charges one fee per team, not per person. The amount depends on the
membership tier the team selects when registering:

- **IEEE RAS member** — the lowest rate
- **IEEE member** — the middle rate
- **Not a member** — the standard rate

The current amounts are shown on the [registration page](/register). An
early-bird discount may be run for a limited period; where there is one, the
discounted price and its end date are shown on that page.

The price your team sees at the moment you register is the price you pay. If
prices are changed afterwards, or an early-bird period ends, your quoted amount
does not change.

To qualify for a member rate, each member claiming it must supply a valid IEEE
or IEEE RAS membership number at registration. These are checked. A team that
cannot substantiate the tier it selected is asked to pay the difference before
it is confirmed.

## How to pay

Payment is by **CliQ** transfer to the chapter's account. The alias, account
name and bank are shown on your payment page once you have registered. Nothing
else is an accepted method — cash, cards, and transfers to any account other
than the one shown on that page are not taken.

Please check the alias character by character before sending. A CliQ transfer to
the wrong alias goes to a stranger and cannot be recovered.

## Confirming your payment

After you have transferred the fee:

1. Enter the transaction reference your banking app gave you.
2. Upload a screenshot of the confirmation from your banking app.
3. Submit.

Both are required. The reference alone cannot be matched reliably against a bank
statement, and a screenshot alone gives nothing to search for.

## Verification takes 24–48 hours

CliQ gives the receiving account no automatic notification that names your team.
Every payment is therefore matched by hand against the chapter's bank statement.
That takes **24 to 48 hours** on a working day, and longer over a weekend or a
public holiday.

You do not need to do anything while you wait, and you do not need to send the
proof again. You are emailed when your payment is verified.

If your payment does not match — a different amount, a reference that cannot be
found, a screenshot that cannot be read — it is marked as needing attention and
you are emailed to explain what is needed.

## Refunds

**Before registration closes.** If you withdraw your team before the
registration deadline, the fee is refunded in full. Email the organizing
committee from the address you registered with.

**After registration closes.** Withdrawals after the deadline are not refunded.
By then your place has been counted into the schedule, the brackets and the
catering, and it cannot be resold.

**If your team cannot be given a place.** If the competition fills, or the event
is cancelled, or a team that has paid otherwise cannot be seated, that team is
refunded in full, regardless of timing.

**If the event is postponed.** Your registration carries over to the new date.
If you cannot attend the new date, say so within 14 days of the announcement and
you are refunded in full.

**No-shows.** A team that is confirmed but does not appear on competition day is
not refunded.

**Overpayments.** If you transfer more than your quoted fee, say so and the
difference is returned.

Refunds go back to the account the payment came from, by CliQ, normally within
14 days of a refund being agreed.

## Errors in the fee quoted

If the wrong amount is quoted or charged, you are told and it is corrected. If
you were undercharged, you are not obliged to make up the difference — the price
shown is honoured.

## Contact

Anything about a payment: reply to your registration confirmation email, quoting
your payment code, and it is picked up by the committee.
